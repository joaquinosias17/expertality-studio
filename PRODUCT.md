# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) on Vercel, code in a private GitHub repo. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

- Franchise brand marketing teams (US brands with 10 to 300 locations) who must keep every location's local posts on brand and legally correct.
- Franchise location owners who are responsible for their own local store marketing and have little time for it. They mostly use a phone.
- The landing page sells to both equally.

## Product Purpose

Expertality Studio lets every location of a franchise create, approve and schedule on-brand local content (reels, posts, Google posts) in minutes, in English and Spanish. The brand sets its rules, templates and campaigns once; stores produce on their own. Success: stores post every week, and the brand approves without asking for changes.

## Positioning

The brand's own guidelines become rules the app checks on every post, each rule linked to its page in the guide. Videos are motion graphics built from the brand's own assets and templates designed once per brand, with licensed music chosen per video, no people on camera needed. Bilingual by design, not translated. Built by a team that ran local marketing for hundreds of franchise locations.

## Operating Context

- Brand team: desktop, uploads the brand guide PDF, logo files, fonts, photos and approved ads; approves templates and flagged posts.
- Store owner: phone, approves the week's drafts in one sitting, adds local ideas.
- Calendar has three layers: brand campaigns, local moments, always-on service posts. Drafts arrive two weeks ahead.
- Publishing to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Business Profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prototype stage. Video engine and rule checks exist as prototypes; publishing integrations are planned, not live.
- Prices are not decided; use placeholders.
- Expertality Local is the done-for-you service on the same app.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Expertality Studio. Part of the Expertality brand; the Expertality design system (midnight, ink, red accent used small, white and cloud; Archivo and Inter; square corners; target lines; precise rules) is binding.
- Voice: short, plain, specific, no hype, no long dashes. Start from the customer's problem.
- English for the US market.

## Evidence on Hand

- No customers, testimonials, logos or metrics yet. Never invent them.
- Founder's prior experience running local marketing for franchise locations may be described without naming clients.
- Sample brand for demos: Mesa Coffee Co. (fictional), labeled as sample.

## Product Principles

1. The brand's rules are law; the app never ships what the guide would reject.
2. Real brand material beats generated decoration.
3. A store owner finishes the week in minutes from a phone.
4. Every claim on the page is true today.
